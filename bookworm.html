BookWorm App

UI/UX Design User Research Figma 2024

Project Overview

BookWorm is an application dedicated to children, designed to foster a love for reading and book exploration. It offers a wide variety of categories through an attractive, interactive interface tailored for kids.

The Problem

Children who enjoy reading books and listening to stories often face a challenge in finding a comprehensive platform that fully meets their specific needs.

The Solution

BookWorm offers the perfect solution by combining text, picture, and audiobooks. It features diverse categories and interactive interfaces with a cheerful, engaging design for children.
